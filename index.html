Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join Pixels & Panels? keyboard_arrow_down

Any student enrolled in the IIT Madras BS Degree program can join! Simply click the "Join Our Community" link or follow our social channels on Instagram and LinkedIn for active onboarding forms.

What kind of events do you organize? keyboard_arrow_down

We organize open-air anime screenings, film discussions, panel breakdowns, cinematography workshops, and cosplay meetups throughout each academic term.

Can I propose a movie or anime for screening? keyboard_arrow_down

Yes! We actively take community proposals. Click the "Suggest Movie/Anime" button in the menu or hero section to submit your recommendation to the core team.
