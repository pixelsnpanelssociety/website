WHO WE ARE

Pixels & Panels is the definitive home for cinema buffs, anime fans, and visual art enthusiasts at the IIT Madras BS Degree Programme. We create a space where students connect across batches, share perspectives, and celebrate storytelling.

flag

OUR MISSION

To bridge technical rigor with creative passion — giving students a vibrant platform to screen masterpiece cinema, debate narrative themes, learn visual editing concepts, and host inclusive community gatherings.
